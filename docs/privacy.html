NutriVerseAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

NutriVerseAI is a nutrition and kitchen management app that runs inside Anna. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

What the app stores

NutriVerseAI has no accounts, passwords or email sign-up of its own. You use it through your Anna account. The app stores the information you enter:

  • Recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, pantry items and kitchen equipment.
  • Nutrition goals and the meals you log against them.
  • Cooking journal and mood entries.
  • Cultural cuisine notes and recipes you create.
  • Your display name, picture, theme and accent colour, and dietary preferences.

Where it is stored

This information is saved in Anna's storage for your account, using Anna's app storage service. It is not sent to a server run by the NutriVerseAI developer, and the developer cannot read it through the app. Anna's own privacy policy applies to how Anna stores and protects it.

If the app is opened outside Anna, it keeps your data in your browser's local storage on your device instead.

How AI features work

When you ask for AI help, such as a meal plan, recipe details, nutrition insights or equipment advice, the app sends the relevant text (for example your preferences, ingredients or recent meals) to Anna's AI service. The answer comes back through Anna and counts against your Anna AI usage. The app does not contain any AI provider keys and does not send this data anywhere else. Anna's terms and privacy policy cover how the AI service handles your request.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, show ads, or build profiles for advertising. The app does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any entry in the app at any time.
  • In Settings, "Delete account" removes all NutriVerseAI data stored for you.
  • Uninstalling the app from Anna stops it from accessing your data. Ask Anna about deleting data that remains in your account.

Health information

The nutrition figures, meal plans and suggestions in the app are for general information. They are not medical advice. Check with a qualified professional before changing your diet for a health condition.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
